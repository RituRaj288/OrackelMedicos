About Oracle Medicos

Your Dependable Pharmaceutical & Medical Products Trading Partner

Oracle Medicos is a dedicated commercial trading enterprise founded in July 2023 in Imphal west, Manipur, India. We specialize in wholesale distribution, sourcing, and commercial supply of pharmaceuticals, medical equipment, surgical products, and healthcare consumables.

Operating as an independent private enterprise in the trading sector, our core mission is to connect certified pharmaceutical and surgical manufacturers with healthcare providers, clinical laboratories, nursing homes, and retail pharmacies across Manipur with transparent pricing and dependable fulfillment.
